# Code of Conduct

Clock Advanced aims to be a practical, welcoming open-source project for Home Assistant users and contributors.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific and patient with people of different experience levels.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Keep issue reports reproducible and relevant to the project.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's entity IDs, notification targets, diagnostics, access details or household information.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope while remaining free to propose alternatives constructively.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate disruption, doxxing, credential sharing and publishing private Home Assistant data are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews and other spaces where someone represents Clock Advanced. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close conversations, reject contributions or restrict participation when necessary to protect the project and its community.

For a moderation concern, contact the maintainer privately through an available GitHub channel. If no private channel is available, open a neutral issue requesting private contact without naming people or publishing allegations or sensitive details. Security vulnerabilities must follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
